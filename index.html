About Biskit Box

Biskit Box started with something I noticed. A lot of the biscuits I bought locally tasted like they had been sitting around for weeks, and even the bread I picked up was plain and tasteless. I wanted food that actually tastes like itself, so I decided to make it fresh. We're a young biscuit startup based in Lahore, Pakistan, with a small range of chocolate chip, salty and creamy biscuits. Every batch is meant to be fresh, simple and fairly priced.
